MP SELECTOR 1.3.0
For Akai MPC Sample running firmware 1.4.0.8

Pick what runs by its cover. Turn the dial on your MPC and press it.
The download has one cover, Akai. It does not install any other firmware.

1. Prepare your microSD
   Use a spare exFAT card. It can have any name. Back up any files you
   want to keep. If the card already has an autorun.sh, autorun folder or
   firmware folder, use another card for this first install. Replacing an
   existing launcher can disable it.

   Unzip MP Selector on your computer. Open "SD Card Files" and copy its three
   items to the top level of the card: autorun.sh, autorun and firmware.
   When you open the card, you should see those three items side by side.

2. Open the selector
   Eject the card from your computer. On the MPC, save your current project.
   Hold Shift and press Pad 8 to open MIDI Config. Under Dev Enhancements
   (Beta), enable Developer Mode. Insert the card and choose Yes when Akai
   asks to run its autorun script. Turn the dial to browse and press it to open.

What to press
   B1 switches between Cover Flow and Library. B2 shows Favorites or all
   entries. B3 stars the current entry. Open an entry or confirm shutdown to
   save a change to your favorites.

   In Cover Flow, a Power tap opens Shutdown. B1 cancels. B3 confirms.

Add firmware
   MP Selector does not include other firmware. Download one from its maker
   and unzip it. In the card's "firmware" folder, make a new folder with the
   name you want to see on the MPC. Copy everything from the download into
   that folder, as it is. It runs the way it would alone on a card.

   For a cover, add a square picture named cover.png or cover.jpg to the
   same folder.

   Firmware that changes the Akai app itself was made for Akai 1.3 and does
   not run on 1.4. Its cover says "Requires Akai firmware 1.3.0.12".

   The list of firmware tried so far is at philwoolley.com/mpselector

   For firmware makers: https://github.com/fomoPhil/mpselector

If you see the stock sampler
   Check the exact Akai version and the Developer Mode switch. From stock,
   with your work saved and card activity finished, reinsert the prepared card.
   If no prompt appears, check that autorun.sh is at the top of the card.

To go back to stock
   Choose Akai in the selector and decline the card script if asked again.
   You can also shut down, remove the selector card and turn the MPC back on.
   MP Selector runs from microSD. Nothing is installed on the MPC.
